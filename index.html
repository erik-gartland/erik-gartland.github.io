Research Analyst

Center on Budget and Policy Priorities

Researcher focused on federal rental assistance, housing affordability, and the policy gaps that leave low-income families without stable housing.

I study the structural causes of housing instability in the United States, with a particular focus on federal rental assistance programs — including Housing Choice Vouchers and project-based Section 8. My work examines how funding limitations, eligibility structures, and administrative barriers shape who receives help and who doesn't.

Selected Work

Publications

2026
Report Funding Limitations Cause Large Unmet Need for Federal Rental Assistance

Center on Budget and Policy Priorities

Only 1 in 4 eligible low-income households receive federal rental assistance. This report documents how discretionary funding constraints — not eligibility rules — determine who gets help, leaving millions of families on waitlists or without assistance entirely.

2024
Report Families Wait Years for Housing Vouchers Due to Inadequate Funding

Center on Budget and Policy Priorities

An analysis of housing authority waitlist data showing that eligible families routinely wait three to seven years for a voucher — a direct consequence of chronic underfunding of the Housing Choice Voucher program.

2022
Report Funding Limitations Create Widespread Unmet Need for Rental Assistance

Center on Budget and Policy Priorities · February 2022

An earlier assessment of the structural gap between rental assistance need and program funding, examining how the discretionary nature of voucher appropriations leaves the most vulnerable renters unserved.
